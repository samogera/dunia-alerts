Live early warnings for Nairobi schools

Dunia Alerts watches air quality, heat, heavy rain and rivers around pilot schools, up to 48 hours ahead, and helps head teachers act in time.

A Dunia Endelevu pilot, led by Alana Chelangat. Data: Open-Meteo (weather, Copernicus CAMS air, GloFAS rivers), OpenAQ sensors when connected. Air bands: US EPA AQI (2024).
