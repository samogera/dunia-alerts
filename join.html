What your school gets

  • Warnings for air, heat, heavy rain and rivers around your school, up to 48 hours ahead
  • One clear step for the head teacher, by WhatsApp or SMS
  • Ready-made messages for parents in English and Kiswahili
  • Free. No app to install, and it works on any phone

What we ask of you

  • A named contact who can reply when a warning comes
  • A short chat with the Dunia team before you start
  • Permission to record how the school responds, never children’s names

What happens next

  1. We read your reply. Every reply reaches the Dunia team.
  2. We get in touch by email or phone for a short chat.
  3. We set up your school on the dashboard and agree who gets the alerts.

The pilot starts small, so schools may join in stages.

Not a school?

County and national offices, NGOs, researchers and funders: use the same form and choose who you are. We would love to work with you.
