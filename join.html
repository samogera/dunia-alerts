Join the Dunia Alerts pilot

For schools in Nairobi that want early warnings for dirty air, extreme heat, heavy rain and rising rivers, and for partners, county offices and funders who want to work with us.

  • About 2 minutes
  • Free for schools
  • Seen only by the Dunia team

What happens next
